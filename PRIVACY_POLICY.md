# Privacy Policy — ShadeSync

**Last updated:** March 22, 2026

## Overview
ShadeSync is an augmented reality (AR) lipstick try-on app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data ShadeSync accesses and how it is used.

## Data We Access

### Camera
ShadeSync uses your device's front-facing camera **solely** for real-time face detection and AR lipstick overlay rendering. Camera frames are processed **entirely on-device** using Google MediaPipe Face Landmarker. **No camera data is transmitted, stored, or sent to any server.**

### Photos
When you tap "Capture," a single screenshot is saved to your device's local photo gallery. This image stays on your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- **No personal data** is collected, stored, or transmitted
- **No facial recognition** — we detect facial landmarks for AR rendering only; no identity information is derived
- **No analytics or tracking**
- **No network requests** — ShadeSync works entirely offline
- **No accounts or sign-ups**
- **No third-party advertising SDKs**

## On-Device Processing
All face detection, skin tone analysis, and shade recommendation processing happens **locally on your device**. Nothing leaves your phone.

## Third-Party Libraries
- **Google MediaPipe** — on-device face landmark detection (no cloud processing)
- **AndroidX CameraX** — camera access framework

Both libraries process data locally and do not transmit data externally.

## Children's Privacy
ShadeSync does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since no data is collected from any user, this is inherently satisfied.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy. Changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this document.

## Full Description (4000 chars max)

💄 ShadeSync — Your Personal Beauty Assistant

Try on 50+ real lipstick shades from top brands like MAC, Nykaa, Maybelline, Charlotte Tilbury, Fenty Beauty, Rare Beauty, and Lakme — all in real-time AR using your front camera.

✨ KEY FEATURES

🎨 50+ Real Lipstick Shades
Browse shades from 8 major brands including MAC Ruby Woo, Charlotte Tilbury Pillow Talk, Fenty Beauty Uncensored, and more. Search by name, brand, or enter any HEX code to try custom colours.

🪞 Realistic AR Rendering
Multi-layer lipstick rendering with edge feathering, depth contours, and specular highlights. Toggle between Matte and Glossy finishes. Adjust brightness and warm/cool tones.

🧠 Smart Skin Analysis
Tap "Analyze Me" to detect your skin tone (Fair to Deep) and undertone (Warm/Cool/Neutral). Get personalised shade recommendations ranked by how well they'll suit you.

💅 Full Look Presets
One-tap curated looks: Date Night, Korean Glass Skin, Office Nude, Berry Glam, Sunset Glow, Soft Rosé, No-Makeup Makeup, and Bold Power. Each combines lipstick, blush, and skin smoothing.

📸 Save & Share
Capture your look and save to gallery or share directly to Instagram, WhatsApp, and more.

🔒 100% PRIVATE
Everything runs on-device. No data collected. No internet required. No accounts. No ads.

⚡ POWERED BY
• Google MediaPipe Face Landmarker (478 facial landmarks)
• CameraX for reliable camera performance
• On-device skin analysis — no cloud processing
